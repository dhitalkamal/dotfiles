argument-hint: "<the decision or question>"

Convene a Council on: $ARGUMENTS

Use a Council only when there are 2 or more valid paths with genuine uncertainty
AND the decision is meaningfully irreversible. Council is expensive. For a clear
domain and a reversible decision, do not convene - just decide or use a single
specialist. If the trigger is not met, say so and stop.

Mandatory anchor voters (always participate regardless of domain):
- strategist
- devils-advocate
Add relevant domain specialists (for example researcher) when the decision needs
outside evidence.

Round 1 - parallel positions (isolated, no cross-influence):
Spawn all council members at once in a single message so they run concurrently.
Brief each like a smart colleague: what is already known, what is ruled out,
what decision the output informs, and the risk level. Each returns a position
with VERDICT and confidence. Do not let them see each other's answers.

Round 2 - cross-examination:
Give each member the others' positions and have them challenge the reasoning.
They may revise their verdict and must say what changed their mind.

Round 3 - vote with mandatory dissent:
Tally the verdicts. Record the dissent explicitly - the dissent is as valuable
as the consensus, it names what we are choosing to ignore.

Then synthesize: one recommendation with confidence, the vote tally, and the
recorded dissent. If the decision is irreversible (L3 or L4 on the autonomy
matrix), present it for confirmation - do not execute.
